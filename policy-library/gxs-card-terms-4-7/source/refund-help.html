How can I request a refund for unauthorised transactions on my GXS Credit Card?

  1. Last updated
  2. Save as PDF
No headers

You may raise a dispute for unauthorised transactions directly through the GXS Bank app.

• Navigate to the FlexiCredit tile
• Select the specific transaction
• Tap 'Get Help'
• Choose 'Raising a card transaction dispute'.
