           Specific Requirement 1:
           Faster Payments APP scam
           reimbursement rules
           Notice of maximum reimbursement level value
1.1        This Notice is published in accordance with paragraph 4.5 of Specific Requirement 1 1 and
           sets out the amount of the maximum level of reimbursement established at paragraph 4.4
           of that Specific Requirement:

             • The maximum level of reimbursement is set at £85,000.
             • The maximum level of reimbursement will not automatically change in line with
               inflation or any other metric.
             • The maximum level of reimbursement will be kept under review by the PSR.

1.2        This Notice comes into force on 7 October 2024.

1.3        This Notice will continue in force until such time as it is varied or revoked by the PSR.
