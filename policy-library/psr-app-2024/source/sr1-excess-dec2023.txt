           Specific Requirement 1:
           Faster Payments APP scam
           reimbursement rules
           Notice of maximum excess value
1.1        Paragraph 5.16 of Specific Requirement 1 1 permits sending payment service providers
           (PSPs) to apply an excess amount to reimbursable Faster Payments APP scam claims.
           This notice sets out the maximum value of that excess:

            • Sending PSPs may apply an excess up to a maximum of £100 per claim.
            • Sending PSPs may decide to apply the maximum excess (£100), a lower excess
              or no excess.

            • The maximum excess value will not automatically change in line with inflation
              or any other metric.

1.2       This notice comes into force on 7 October 2024.

1.3       This notice shall continue in force until such time as it is varied or revoked by the PSR.
